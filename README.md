## 🔬 Research
- **Grounded but Not Yet Correct: A Pre-registered, Source-Verified Evaluation of Small Language Models for Meal Planning** *(solo author, 2026)*: a pre-registered evaluation of four 3–4B models across 1,600 runs grounded in a 542-food dataset, with a deterministic verifier that rechecks every nutrient and constraint claim. Retrieval sharply cuts nutrient errors, but 30–43% of plans still fail for reasons numeric correction can't fix.
- **Robust ML-Based CPU–GPU Device Selection Under Hardware and Load Shift** *(first author, with Dr. Palak Goyal and Dr. Shruti Arora, 2026)*: 7,000 measurements across 7 workloads on 3 hardware/load settings, evaluated by latency-charged regret against threshold and roofline baselines. A cost-aware model cuts regret from 3.8% to 1.6% on a laptop, online bandits halve regret after a hardware change, and the simple rule still wins on a Tesla T4. *Code and data will be released with the paper.*
